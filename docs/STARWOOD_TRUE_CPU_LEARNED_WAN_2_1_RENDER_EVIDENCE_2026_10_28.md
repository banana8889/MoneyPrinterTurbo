## Executive status
The earlier audit that said "no local learned inference" is superseded: on the free ARM64 machine with 4 CPU cores and ~8GiB RAM, the open-source `stable-diffusion.cpp` executable **was actually compiled** with its upstream GGML, WebP and related submodules, and was run with the real **Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B Q4** checkpoint, UMT5 XXL Q4 text encoder, and Wan VAE. ### Evaluation & boundaries
This proves that small novel samples **can** be generated by a trained neural video model on a CPU-only instance. It does NOT prove cinematic quality, long-form continuity, consistent acting, lip sync or *three seven-minute episodes daily*. On this hardware, even the short low-quality 0.75-second proof took over four minutes. Do not claim realtime performance or make up new scenes. Quality control rejects the samples for release.
